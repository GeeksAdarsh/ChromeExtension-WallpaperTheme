Minimal New Tab Wallpaper — Quiet Stars

A quiet new tab. Tiny stars, a clock, and Google search. Each shuffle switches from a dark wallpaper to a light one.

How to use it

  1. Open a new tab

    The clock, date, and greeting are already there. Click your name to change it.

  2. Change the wallpaper

    Click the shuffle mark at the bottom right. One click is a dark sky. The next is a light sky. The circle shows only while you hover.

  3. Search

    Type and press Enter for Google. A web address, such as mail.google.com, opens that site.

  4. Bookmarks

    Mail is first. Add sits beside it. Hide puts every bookmark away, including Add. Bookmarks brings them back.

  5. City and notes

    City is one line of weather. Drag it anywhere. × hides it and keeps the city. Note is a list: tick a point when it is done, Hide closes the card and keeps the list, and × on a point deletes only that point.

  6. Focus

    Focus leaves the clock, search, and wallpaper mark, and hides the rest. Exit brings them back.
