Minimal New Tab Wallpaper — Quiet Stars

A quiet new tab. A clock, Google search, notes by day, a job counter, a study timer, and 130 wallpapers, including hand-drawn colored-pencil art.

How to use it

  1. Open a new tab

    The clock, date, and greeting are already there. Click your name to change it.

  2. Change the wallpaper

    Click the shuffle mark at the bottom right for a random wallpaper. Hover it to see Auto beside it: click Auto to get a new wallpaper on each tab, once a day, or light by day and dark at night.

  3. Search

    Type and press Enter for Google. A web address, such as mail.google.com, opens that site.

  4. Bookmarks

    Mail is first. Add sits beside it. Hide puts every bookmark away, including Add. Bookmarks brings them back.

  5. City and notes

    City is one line of weather. Drag it anywhere. × hides it and keeps the city. Notes have a tab for each day, and a new day starts at 4 AM. + makes your own list, such as Special. Drag tabs and notes to reorder them. The bell on a note brings it back into Today later. Count applied jobs with + and −, and see the week, your streak, and your best run. Click the counter name to rename it, and + Profile adds another counter. Type a word before a link, or click + label, to say what the link is for.

  6. Study

    Study turns the big clock into a countdown. Everything else still works. The minutes count toward your streak.

  7. Focus

    Focus leaves the clock, search, and wallpaper mark, and hides the rest. Exit brings them back.

Backup

Save your notes, lists, job counts, study time, bookmarks, and settings to one file. Restore it on another computer, or after Chrome is reset.
